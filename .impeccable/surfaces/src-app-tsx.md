Scope: whole product (Home = Persuade, Lab = Operate, Experiments/Learn/Methodology = Read). Audience: bioprocess students and educators at a laptop or projector.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Contemporary research software played straight (the user chose the category standard; Benchling/Observable/Linear-level craft is the bar). Refuses the dark neon HUD dashboard it replaces.
OWN-WORLD: Neutral light ground #F6F6F4, white surfaces, hairline rules instead of cards and shadows, charcoal ink that also carries primary actions and selected states (Linear-style), with one restrained teal accent reserved for the simulation Run action, links, focus rings and the caret, a validated colour per simulated quantity (X ochre, S blue, P berry, mu green, V violet). Schibsted Grotesk UI/display, Red Hat Mono for measurements, STIX Two for mathematics. Small radii, no glow, no glass, no gradients.
STORY: The visitor sees the real vessel running on a light bench, understands it is a simulation driven by equations, and enters the Lab to configure, simulate, observe and analyze.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: left, a large grotesk headline ("Watch a culture grow, starve or wash out."), lede, Open the Lab primary action, then a live readout row and a live X/S figure from the demo run; right, the 3D vessel at full height on a pale bench. Lab: toolbar (experiment picker as h1, reactor configuration selector with schematics, baseline/export/classroom), sticky Configure inspector left, vessel + transport centre, Observe measurements right.
FORM: Category standard (canon), seed cdf3d361.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
